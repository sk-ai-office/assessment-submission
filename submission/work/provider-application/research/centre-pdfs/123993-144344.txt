The Space Development Expression of Interest for Municipalities is an initiative to 
support increasing the number of regulated child care centre spaces in the province. This 
is intended for municipalities to develop new or additional regulated child care sites or 
create additional spaces in existing facilities that will result in new child care spaces.

If you have not completed a Community Needs Assessment or the most recent 
Community Needs Assessment was conducted prior to April 1, 2023, you will need to 
complete this and submit it along with this Expression of Interest.
