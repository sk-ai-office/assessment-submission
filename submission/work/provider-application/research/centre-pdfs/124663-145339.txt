Purpose of the Parent Handbook

The Parent Handbook sets out the policies and procedures for the operation of a child care centre. The Parent Handbook assists parents to understand the child care service and serves to guide expectations of the parents of children who attend the centre. 

The policies and procedures in the Parent Handbook establish how the child care service may be used. Several policies and procedures are developed as requirements of child care legislation. Other policies and procedures may be developed that are unique to the operation of this child care centre.

Legislation
Requirement: A licensee must make available to parents of children attending the facility a copy of The Child Care Act, 2014 and The Child Care Regulations, 2015.
(refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 39 for further information).

Philosophy Statement
Requirement: A written philosophy statement must be developed for the centre (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 14 for further information).

Daily Schedule
Requirement: The centre must plan and implement a daily program that is consistent with the philosophy statement and is developmentally appropriate for children attending the centre (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 14 for further information).  

Child Management
Requirement: A licensee must implement child management practices that enable children to learn independence and socially appropriate behaviour and teaches 
self-discipline rather than disciplining the children. The following practices are not permitted forms of child management: corporal punishment, physical, emotional, or verbal abuse, denial of necessities, isolation, inappropriate physical or mechanical restraint (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 15 for further information).

Program Operation
Requirement: A licensee must develop written policies and procedures for the operation for the child care centre (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part II – Section 7 and Part III – Section 16 for further information).

Enrolment
Requirement: A licensee must enter into a written agreement with a parent for the provision of child care services (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 17 for further information).

Changes to Child Care Services
Requirement: A licensee must enter into a written agreement with a parent for the provision of child care services. Agreements are updated as changes occur (e.g. change in fees or hours of care). Refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 17 for further information).

Termination of Child Care Services
Requirement: A licensee must enter into a written agreement with a parent for the provision of child care services (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 17 for further information).

Fee Schedule and Payment
Requirement: A licensee must have a written schedule of fees and give advance notice to parents of any changes to fees. Parents are charged the same fees for the same service (e.g. the infant child care fee is the same for all parents with infants). Refer to the Child Care Licensee’s  Manual – Part III – Section 18 for further information.

Meals and Snacks
Requirement: Facilities must serve meals and snacks to children who are six months of age or older that meet their nutritional needs and ensure children are fed appropriately according to their ages and developmental level. The centre is not required to supply infant formula or baby food. Children must be served a meal or snack at a minimum of three hour intervals. Menus follow Canada’s Food Guide and are posted for parents (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 24 for further information).

Health Policies
Requirement: If a licensee suspects a child has a Category I or II communicable disease, the licensee must inform a designated public health officer and follow any recommendations to ensure the health and well-being of children attending the centre (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 26 for further information).

Administration of Medication 
Requirement: 
(1) Written Authorization: If a licensee agrees to administer medication, written authorization on a form supplied by the ministry must be obtained. On the same form, a written record of each dose of medication must be made. All non-emergency medications must be stored in a locked enclosure (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s  Manual – Part III – Section 27(1) for further information).
(2) Oral Authorization: In exceptional circumstances, a licensee may administer non-prescription medication on the oral authorization of the parent of the child. Written authorization must be obtained as soon as possible in these circumstances (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 27(2) for further information).

Injuries and Unusual Occurrences 
Requirement: If a child in a regulated child care facility sustains an injury or is involved in an unusual or unexpected occurrence, it is a requirement to notify parents/guardians, ministry staff and submit a written report to the parent/guardian, Ministry of Education and the board of directors (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Division 3 – Section 34 for further information).

Confidentiality
Requirement: All facilities must maintain the confidentiality of personal information of children attending the centre, including files, documents, or any other records. Confidential information must not be disclosed without a parent’s written permission, except as required for the health and safety of children, or as required by law (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part III – Section 40 for further information).

Child Care Centre Staff 
Requirements: The licensee of a centre must appoint a centre director, supervisor and qualified child care workers (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part IV – Division 1 for further information).

Supervision
Requirement: A licensee of a centre must ensure that children are adequately supervised at all times (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part IV – Division 2 for further information).

Physical Environment & Equipment 
Requirement: The physical environment must promote the safety and well-being of children (refer to the Child Care Licensee’s Manual – Part IV – Division 3 for further information).

Governance  
Requirement: The licensee should ensure parents/guardians have knowledge of the centre’s board structure, governance and management of the child care centre.
