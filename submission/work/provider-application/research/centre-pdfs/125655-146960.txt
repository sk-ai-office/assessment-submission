Responsible to: 
• The Board of Directors 
 
Objective: 
• To ensure the provision of a quality child care service which fulfills the philosophy of the centre. 
• To ensure that the centre operates in compliance with The Child Care Act, 2014 and The Child 
Care Regulations, 2015. 
• To administer the policies of the Board by developing and implementing procedures which meet 
the goals and objectives of the centre. 
• To provide key information to the Board to assist them in their decision and policy making 
function. 
 
Duties and Responsibilities: 
• Administration 
o Maintain accurate recordkeeping (ex. children’s files, staff files, billings and fee 
collections, receipts, attendance records, banking) as required for the operation of the 
organization. 
o Assist in the preparation of the annual operating budget. 
o Complete monthly financial requirements such as billings, payables, receivables. 
o Work with the bookkeeper to ensure the completion of accurate monthly financial 
statements for the review of the Board. 
o Purchase services, supplies and equipment as needed according to the operating 
budget. 
o Research and apply for additional sources of funding. 
o Complete annual licensing requirements. 
o Develop procedures to ensure implementation of licensing requirements and board 
policies. 
o Maintain enrollment through advertisement and the delivery of a quality program. 
• Human Resources 
o Recruit qualified staff members. 
o Arrange staff scheduling to ensure ratios are maintained. 
o Provide new staff members and volunteers with an orientation to the organization. 
o Conduct regular performance reviews. 
o Implement appropriate disciplinary action as required. 
o Encourage personal and professional growth of staff members through the provision of 
regularly scheduled staff meetings, in-service training, professional development 
opportunities and a resource centre. 
o Accept, provide support and evaluate ECE practicum students. 
o Manage human resources in compliance with applicable regulatory bodies (ex. 
Employment Standards, Occupational Health and Safety, Worker’s Compensation).

• Program/Child Development 
o Ensure the overall safety and wellbeing of children enrolled at the centre. 
o Ensure the implementation of a developmentally appropriate program through the 
review of program plans, and the active participation and observation of program 
activities. 
o Ensure the implementation of an inclusive program and environment that meets the 
needs of all children. 
o Oversee menu planning. 
o Positive role model. 
• Family Support 
o Provide families with an orientation to the centre when enrolling. 
o Encourage parental involvement on the board of directors, in fundraising, children’s 
program and work bees. 
o Ensure effective communication with families through the implementation of a variety 
of methods (ex. newsletters, parent bulletin boards, verbal communication). 
o Assist families in connecting with agencies and resources as required. 
o Address parent concerns in a timely manner. 
• Professional and Community Involvement 
o Encourage and support staff participation in professional and community organizations. 
o Participate in relevant professional committees and organizations. 
o Model professional behaviour for staff. 
• Board Relations 
o Attend all board meetings in an advisory capacity. 
o Provide a written report on the status of the centre to the Board at each board meeting 
and to the membership annually. 
o Provide background information on issues as required by the Board for policy 
development. 
o Provide policy drafts for board approval. 
o Assist the Board in the planning of committee, board, and annual general meetings. 
o Ensure that the centre meets all legal requirements. 
o Inform Board of maintenance, renovation and equipment requirements. 
o Conduct an annual program evaluation to be provided to the Board. 
o Inform the Board of government initiatives which affect the delivery of child care. 
o Act as a liaison between the board and staff or parents to ensure all concerns or 
complaints are addressed.
