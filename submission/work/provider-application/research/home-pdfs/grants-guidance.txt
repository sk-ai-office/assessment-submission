What funding is available to assist with the costs of Family Child Care Home licensing? 
There are two types of grants available to assist Family Child Care Homes with the costs of developing 
and licensing new child care spaces: The Fire, Health and Safety Grant and the Start-Up Grant. 
Fi
re, Health and Safety Grant 
This is a one-time grant to assist new Family Child Care Homes with costs associated with meeting the 
fire, health and safety requirements of The Child Care Act, 2014 and The Child Care Regulations, 2015, 
including the requirements resulting from fire inspections. This may involve purchasing equipment or 
making necessary renovations to a residential premises.  
NO
TE: The licensee is responsible for meeting any municipal requirements and completing other 
inspections as required. 
What
 are some examples of acceptable expenditures? 
See page two for examples of acceptable expenditures. 
All ap
plications for the Fire, Health and Safety Grant must be accompanied by the written cost estimates 
for the items. Copies of fire inspections or written communication from the fire inspector may be 
requested to verify that an item is required as a result of an inspection. 
What
 costs are excluded from this fund? 
Equipment not required to meet fire, health or safety requirements or not required prior to the 
commencement of licensed operations will not be funded.  
What
 is the maximum amount a Family Child Care Home provider may receive? 
New Family Child Care Homes are eligible to receive a maximum of $2,400  
When i
s payment made? 
Fire, Health and Safety Grants are typically paid to the licensee after the completion of a fire inspection 
prior to the commencement of licensed operations. 
St
art-Up Grant
What is the Start-Up G rant? 
This is a one-time grant to assist Family Child Care Homes with the costs associated with developing new 
child care spaces. 
What are some examples of acceptable expenditures? 
Any costs associated with developing new child care spaces are acceptable. Proposed expenditures 
should be discussed with your Early Learning and Child Care (ELCC) Consultant. 
What
 is the maximum amount a Family Child Care Home provider may receive? 
• New Family Child Care Homes are eligible to receive a maximum of $3,525
($3,975 in the Northern Saskatchewan Administration District)
• New Group Family Child Care Homes are eligible to receive a maximum of $3,725

H
ow Does a Family Child Care Home provider apply for these grants? 
Application forms for Start-Up and Fire, Health and Safety Grants are available from the Early Learning 
and Child Care (ELCC) Consultant who is assisting with the development of the child care spaces. 
Applications should be submitted to the ELCC Consultant. 
Who
 can I call if I have questions about the Start-Up and Fire, Health and Safety Grant? 
Please call your ELCC Consultant for clarification or consultation.
