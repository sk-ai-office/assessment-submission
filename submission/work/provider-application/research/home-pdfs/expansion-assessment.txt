Indoor Space Requirements  
Section 66 of The Child Care Regulations, 2015 requires at least 3.7 square metres of usable floor area per child. 
Please explain how your home will meet the required usable floor space for the proposed number of children.  If other 
people live in or use the home during operating hours, describe how family routines  will be managed during child care 
operating hours to ensure usable floor space in maintained for the children in your care. 
Provide:  
• floor plan or detailed drawing of the home,  
• measurements of all areas identified for child care use, 
• washroom facilities in accordance with Section 22 of The Child Care Regulations, 2015 . 
 
Applications submitted without this information will be rejected.
