Apply to Start a Regulated Family Child Care Home

Regulated family child care homes provide child care services in a personal home. The aim of the Family Child Care Home program is to have young children cared for by a warm, caring person in a safe environment that meets their developmental needs and supports and respects their families.

Under the Canada-Saskatchewan Canada-Wide Early Learning and Child Care Agreement extension, Saskatchewan is adjusting how new child care spaces are approved. The province is shifting from rapid expansion to a focus on long-term sustainability.

This approach allows recently opened programs time to fill their spaces and helps reduce pressure on the child care system ensuring it remains strong and sustainable for the future.

1. Benefits of Regulation

In Saskatchewan, not every child care home needs to be regulated to operate, but regulated homes receive considerable funding and supports from the Governments of Saskatchewan and Canada.

The Ministry of Education welcomes both new and current child care home business owners to apply. If you’re a new business owner, regulated child care can be a fulfilling and profitable career. For current child care providers, regulating your child care home can boost your business and offer you extra support.

Find more information about the benefits of becoming a regulated child care provider.

3. How to Apply for Regulation

Thank you for your interest in becoming a regulated child care home provider. The ministry reviews each application based on local need and contacts all applicants to advise them of next steps. In areas that are already well served, applications may not move forward at this time. If this is the case for your area, your name will be kept on file for up to three months; if a need arises to increase child care spaces in your area, you will be contacted by phone to see if you remain interested.

  1. Complete the online Family Child Care Home Applicant Information Session
    This session will help you decide if operating a regulated child care home is right for you and explains the process in detail. Make sure you are familiar with The Child Care Regulations, 2015.

  2. Submit an application package
    Your full application needs to include the completed:
    • Family Child Care Home Licence Application
    • Criminal Record Check, including a Vulnerable Sector Check, for you and anyone 18 years of age or older living in your home. Visit your local police service to apply for a criminal record check and a vulnerable sector check.
    • Family Assessment Questionnaire
    • Direct Deposit Payment Request Form

  3. Information Sessions
    Applicants who are screened in participate in two information sessions on regulated child care licensing. Applicants work through their Learning Plans toward becoming regulated.

  4. Screening and Home Visitations
    The Ministry of Education will continue to screen applicants and will conduct visits to assess your home as part of the regulatory process. Applicants will be contacted to arrange the visits. During these visits, applicants will have the opportunity to ask questions about child care regulations and the next steps in the process.

    An Early Learning and Child Care Consultant will advise you when to schedule the fire and heating system inspections and what should be included.

  5. Information Sessions and Home Visitations
    Applicants continue to work through their Learning Plans and participate in two final information sessions on regulated child care. Applicants will submit required documents and may begin to apply for multiple grants. A consultant may conduct additional home visitation during this stage.

  6. Licensing Meeting and Granting
    You will receive communication from a consultant informing you of the status of your application. Once you have successfully completed all the steps to becoming regulated, your licence will be mailed or delivered.
