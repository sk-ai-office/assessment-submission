About the Play and Exploration Grant 
The Play and Exploration Grant is a one-time grant provided to regulated child care facilities as a 
component of Saskatchewan’s Action Plan for the Canada-Saskatchewan Canada-Wide Early Learning and 
Child Care Agreement.  Play and Exploration: Early Learning Program Guide is Saskatchewan’s 
foundational early learning framework to guide quality practice and approaches in the early learning sector.  
The intent of the Play and Exploration Grant is to further support the licensed early learning and child care 
sector to implement Play and Exploration in their programs. 
Grant Calculation 
The Play and Exploration Grant is available to child care centres and homes licensed in the following 
amounts: 
• Family Child Care Homes - $600 
• Group Family Child Care Homes - $900 
• Child Care Centres - $2,500 for the ﬁrst 30 spaces and $75 per space for additional spaces. 
 
Examples for centre grant calculation:   
Child Care Centre – 14 spaces $2,500 
Child Care Centre – 30 spaces $2,500 
Child Care Centre – 51 spaces $4,075* 
*$2,500 for the first 30 spaces + $1,575 for the remainder of 
spaces (21 spaces x $75 per space) 
Eligibility Requirements 
Completion. of. Play. and. Exploration. Phase. I.  To be eligible for grant funding, child care home providers 
will need to have participated in the Ministry of Education’s Play and Exploration Phase I workshop. Child 
Care Centres will need to ensure at least 75% of full-time staff will participate in a Play and Exploration 
Phase I workshop within the initial year. 
Child care home providers are required to submit a copy of their Play and Exploration Phase I workshop 
certiﬁcate. Child care centres are required to complete the Phase I workshop summary identifying all full-
time staff and indicate which of these staff have completed the workshop. Centres are also required to 
submit a copy of the Phase I participation certiﬁcates for staff who have completed the workshop online 
and may be completed individually or as a staﬀ group. The Phase I workshop is an introduction to Play 
and Exploration: Early Learning Program Guide, with a focus on creating holistic, responsive 
environments. Further information about how to register for this online workshop can be found here.

Expenditure Guidelines 
Eligible Expenditures 
Items that support the implementation of Play and Exploration indoors and outdoors are eligible 
expenditures. These items may include furniture, equipment, materials and resource books.   
Examples include: 
• low-level shelving accessible to children to deﬁne experiential centres 
• open-faced bookshelves 
• light tables 
• consumable materials, such as art supplies (clay, paper, pencil crayons, pastels) 
• equipment or material to enhance outdoor learning environments (outdoor musical instruments, 
easels, loose parts) 
• quality children’s literature  
• resource books for educators 
 
Ineligible Expenditures 
The following are not eligible expenditures: 
• Attendance at professional development (PD) opportunities. The Ministry of Education’s 
Professional Development Grant currently provides support for PD opportunities.  
• Items and activities unrelated to the implementation of Play and Exploration in the child care 
facility, such as: 
o storage containers and shelving inaccessible to children 
o children’s activities offsite 
o landscaping elements, such as ground cover, gazebos, paving stones, sod 
• Furnishings and equipment which are primarily purchased prior to becoming operational/licensed, 
typically supported by the Ministry of Education’s Start-Up Grant, such as: 
o cribs 
o highchairs 
o lockers and cubbies 
o adult seating  
o child sized tables and chairs 
Grant Planning Supports 
The Ministry of Education has developed an Early Learning Furnishings, Equipment and Materials resource 
which may support you to select items in alignment with the Play and Exploration Grant. The equipment 
and materials that are presented in this resource are relevant to multiple areas of development, and may 
be interconnected in invitations, experiential centres, and projects. Suggestions for experiential centres are 
provided in the resource, as well as links to core documents such as Play and Exploration: Early Learning 
Program Guide, Play and Exploration for Infants and Toddlers, and Essential Learning Experiences.

Application Processes 
To apply for the Play and Exploration Grant, submit an application form to the ministry’s Child Care 
Operations Unit at childcare@gov.sk.ca. Include a copy of your planning template with your application 
form. As you complete the planning template, consider how your planned purchases will impact your early 
learning program. Consider how the purchases will holistically contribute to enhancing the environment 
and the early learning program: 
• How will the purchase contribute to enhancing children’s learning? 
• In what ways can these items respond to children’s current and emergent interests?  
• Are these items ﬂexible?  Can they be integrated into more than one experiential centre? 
• How can children’s early literacy be fostered with the items? How can concepts be introduced or 
expanded? 
• In what ways will a purchase impact an early childhood educator’s practice? 
• How will the items inspire curiosity, inquiry, and investigation? 
• Does the range of purchases support the implementation of Play and Exploration for all age groups 
present in the facility (infants, toddlers, preschoolers)? 
• How will the facility measure the ways the items are impacting children’s early learning? 
Submit your Play and Exploration Grant Application, Play and Exploration Phase I Workshop Summary, 
Phase I Workshop Certiﬁcates and a copy of your Play and Exploration Grant Planning Template to 
childcare@gov.sk.ca. Retain receipts of purchases for ministry staff to review upon request.
