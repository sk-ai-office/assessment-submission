Child Care Home Information Release 
 
I understand that the Early Years Branch will release my name, address and telephone 
number to parents seeking child care unless I explicitly request that this is not to be done. 
 
 
 
 
 I hereby give permission to the Early Years Branch to release my name, address, and 
telephone number to persons or groups who may wish to contact me for the purpose of: 
 
 offering assistance, support, or training opportunities; 
 advising of community activities, resources; 
 providing product information that is directly relevant to the provision of child care 
services; 
 conducting a legitimate inquiry in the field of child care, or for any purposes that are 
likely to be of benefit to myself or the child care situation in Saskatchewan. 
  
 I hereby deny permission to the Early Years Branch to release my name, address, and 
telephone number to persons or groups who may wish to contact me for the purpose of: 
 
 offering assistance, support, or training opportunities; 
 advising of community activities, resources; 
 providing product information that is directly relevant to the provision of child care 
services; 
 conducting a legitimate inquiry in the field of child care, or for any purposes that are 
likely to be of benefit to myself or the child care situation in Saskatchewan.
