Section 92 of The Child Care Regulations, 2015, states the Minister may make a grant to a licensee of a facility 
for the reimbursement of tuition fees paid and required books purchased for courses in early childhood 
education that are successfully completed by a person for the purpose of meeting the requirements of these 
regulations, or for the purpose of upgrading his or her qualifications as an early childhood educator.

you may be eligible for tuition reimbursement for the portion not covered by alternative funding, up to $1,500 
per course. International students are not eligible for tuition reimbursement.
