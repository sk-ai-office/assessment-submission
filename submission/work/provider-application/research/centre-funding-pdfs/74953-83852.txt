T
ravel Expenses Information 
• Mileage costs – Calculate mileage at the rate of $0.5499 /km or $0.5922/km in northern Saskatchewan (north of 54th) for the distance from the child care facility to the
event (effective April 1, 2026).
• Accommodation – Hotel expenses (receipts required) can be submitted for two-day events and for events for which the person would otherwise have had to leave home
before 7 a.m. in order to attend.
• Meals – Breakfast @ $16, lunch @ $23, and supper @ $31 (Note: receipts are not required but payment will not be made for meals provided at the event). Meals can only
be claimed if the event/travel requires the individual to be away from home over the typical mealtimes.
St
affing Stipend Information  
Time required by individuals to complete training opportunities may vary but the staffing stipend amount entered on the application must match the staffing stipend identified on 
the Eligible Opportunities for 2026-27 Professional Development Grant Funding document. Facilities may close for up to two professional development days in a licensing year 
(Section 7 of The Child Care Regulations, 2015) but staffing stipends cannot be claimed during those closures. Staffing stipends are determined based on the following:  
• If the event is 1 to 2 hours in length, stipend eligibility is $25
• If the event is a half day event (3-4 hours in length), stipend eligibility is $50
• If the event is a full day event (5-8 hours in length), stipend eligibility is $100
• If the event is multiple full days, eligibility is $100/day

oof of Attendance/Certification
