Active Play Grant Information Sheet 
 
About the Active Play Grant 
The Active Play Grant is a one-time grant provided to licensed child care facilities as a component 
of Saskatchewan’s Action Plan for the Canada-Saskatchewan Canada-Wide Early Learning and 
Child Care Agreement. The intent of Active Play Grant is to encourage active play in licensed 
settings. 
 
Grant Calculation 
• The Active Play Grant is available to child care centres and homes: 
o Family Child Care Homes - $500 
o Group Family Child Care Homes - $750 
o Child Care Centres - $1,900 for the first 30 spaces and $60 per space for additional 
spaces. 
Examples for centre grant calculation:   
Child Care Centre – 14 spaces $1,900 
Child Care Centre – 30 spaces $1,900 
Child Care Centre – 51 spaces $3,160*  
*$1,900 for the first 30 spaces + $1,260 for the remainder of spaces 
(21 spaces x $60 per space) 
 
Eligibility Requirements 
• Child care homes must be licensed and centres must have a developmental licence to be 
eligible to receive funding.   
Expenditure Guidelines 
Eligible Expenditures 
• Items that support active play indoors and outdoors, such as: 
o soft, foam play climbers 
o tunnels, pillows, mats 
o crawl structures 
o stepping stones 
o riding toys that can be pushed with the feet (infants/toddlers) or pedaled 
(preschoolers) 
o balls of various sizes and textures to roll, throw and kick 
o balance beams 
o quality children’s literature that encourages active play 
o resource books for educators that focus on active play 
 
• Active play enhancements to outdoor learning environments, such as: 
o landscaping elements to promote active play, such as hills and pathways

o stages and platforms 
o structures that encourage climbing, sliding, jumping 
 
• Professional development opportunities (outside of the parameters of the Ministry of 
Education Professional Development Grant) that build educator capacity to implement 
active play programming (e.g. creative movement or physical literacy professional learning 
opportunities. 
 
Ineligible Expenditures 
• The following are not eligible expenditures: 
o Attendance at professional development (PD) opportunities that fall within the 
parameters of the Ministry of Education’s Professional Development Grant. 
o Items and activities unrelated to active play. 
Grant Planning Supports 
The Ministry of Education has developed an Active Play Brochure which may support you to 
intentionally plan active play experiences. Links to resources, such as The Canadian 24-Hour 
Movement Guidelines for the Early Years, are provided in the brochure. 
 
Application Processes 
To apply for the Active Play Grant, submit the application form and planning template to the child 
care office at childcare@gov.sk.ca. Retain receipts of purchases for review upon request.
