Professional development (PD) is a valuable investment in staff. It is connected to 
increased engagement at work as well as higher job satisfaction and increased retention 
rates. Supporting the opportunity to learn new skills, gain confidence, stay up to date with 
current research, and build connections with colleagues makes employees feel valued, 
increases performance, and builds capacity.  
 
The 2025-26 Professional Development (PD) Grant is available to regulated child care 
facilities to assist with the cost of professional development expenses, under the following 
terms and conditions:  
• Grants will be issued to the facility for PD opportunities that are completed by anyone 
who is working in the child care facility as well as individuals serving as board members 
of a regulated child care centre.  
• Grants will be issued for completed events identified in the Eligible Opportunities for  
2025-26 Professional Development Grant Funding document. This includes a variety of 
in-person and online sessions and may be updated periodically throughout the year as  
additional events become available. 
 
Every regulated facility is eligible to receive a maximum PD Grant amount for events taking 
place March 15, 2025, to March 15, 2026. Please consider the maximum grant listed below 
when planning (i.e., carpooling or local PD rather than long distance PD).  
 
Centres with 30 or fewer spaces  $1,500  
Centres with 31-60 spaces  $2,500  
Centres with 61-90 spaces  $3,500  
Family Child Care Homes  $300  
Family Child Care Group Homes (provider $300 & assistant $300)  $600  
 
The PD Grant is available for the following expenses related to eligible events.  
 
Event Expenses  
• Registration fees. Receipts are required.  
• Travel costs are eligible, as specified below:  
o Effective October 1, 2025: The mileage rate is $0.5555 per km or $0.5981 per 
km in northern Saskatchewan (north of 54th). Mileage expenses can be 
claimed if travel is required. Distance is calculated from the community where 
the child care facility is located to the community where the professional 
learning event is being held.  
o Meal rates are an eligible expense if a person is required to be away from their 
community during regular mealtimes, and if meals are not provided at the event.

Rates are $16 for breakfast, $23 for lunch and $31 for supper. Receipts are not 
required.  
o Accommodation costs can be claimed for events that are two or more days in 
length or for events when the person would have to leave home before 7:00 a.m. 
to attend. Receipts are required. 
o Travel costs cannot be claimed when an eligible event is held in the community 
in which the individual is employed, or for online events.  
 
Staffing Expenses  
• A staffing stipend is available to help offset the costs of additional wages and/or hiring 
replacement staff: 
o $25 for events 2 hours or less 
o $50 for half-day events 
o $100 for full-day events 
o $100/day for multiple full day events 
• If an individual registers for the entire duration of an event but does not attend the 
entire event, the staffing stipend will be adjusted.  
 
Each facility must determine and communicate to staff how PD event expenses will be 
paid. This includes which staff expenses will be applied for under the PD Grant, which will 
be paid by the facility, and which expenses are the responsibility of the participant. The 
facility may need to set guidelines to allow for equitable opportunities for all staff who 
indicate interest in completing a PD learning opportunity.   
 
The following steps will assist you in the application process:  
1. Employers will complete the Application for Reimbursement of Professional 
Development Expenses for the period of October 1, 2025, to March 15, 2026, for all 
staff/board members who attended an event. More than one application form can be 
submitted but the total of all applications cannot exceed your facility’s maximum PD 
Grant amount.  
 
2. Submit the completed forms to childcare@gov.sk.ca within two months following 
each event. 
 
3. March 15, 2026, is the deadline for applications to be submitted. Applications 
received after the deadline date will not be processed. 
 
To find out more information about Professional Development Grant eligible 
opportunities, please visit the Early Learning Portal.  
 
If you have any questions about the Professional Development Grant, including 
questions about eligibility for an opportunity, please email childcare@gov.sk.ca.
