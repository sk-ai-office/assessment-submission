AI ASSESSMENT AND PROPOSED ACTION
Assessment: Service / policy fix
Opportunity / first intervention: Maintain accurate clinic availability and referral routes first; consider question answering only after an accountable data owner is established.
What AI cannot fix: It cannot recruit clinicians, open patient panels or guarantee ongoing care.
First step: Confirm how clinics report whether they accept patients and how residents are directed when no local option exists.
Success measure: Accuracy of published clinic-availability information when checked with providers.
Why this starting point: The primary constraint is access to clinical capacity. Better navigation helps only when it reflects real availability.
