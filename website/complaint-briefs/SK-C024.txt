CONCERN AND IMPACT
Evacuees report delayed financial assistance, insufficient supplies and unclear updates or return-home instructions.
Delayed aid, insufficient supplies and conflicting instructions increase uncertainty while people are displaced.

AI ASSESSMENT AND PROPOSED ACTION
Assessment: AI + service change
Opportunity / first intervention: Help emergency-support staff draft consistent updates from approved current notices and verified assistance-status records.
What AI cannot fix: It cannot supply aid, authorize payments, determine evacuation safety or issue independent return-home advice.
First step: Trace how an approved notice reaches evacuees and identify conflicting versions. Test staff-reviewed drafts in a tabletop exercise before considering live use.
Success measure: Time from an approved notice to a consistent recipient update.
Why this starting point: Communication is consequential and repeated, but emergency authority, accessible channels and version control must be established first.
