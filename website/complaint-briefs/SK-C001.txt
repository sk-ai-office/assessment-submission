AI ASSESSMENT AND PROPOSED ACTION
Assessment: Service / policy fix
Opportunity / first intervention: Start with bed capacity, patient flow and escalation reviews; no AI pilot is recommended from these complaints alone.
What AI cannot fix: It cannot create beds, staff wards or establish that a care arrangement is clinically appropriate.
First step: Ask clinical operations to identify the measured causes of hallway stays and whether a specific information bottleneck warrants separate investigation.
Success measure: Patient hours spent receiving care in hallways.
Why this starting point: Repeated concerns are serious, but the evidence supports an operational capacity problem rather than a defined AI use case.
