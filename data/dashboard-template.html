How the counts work

One visible account counts once per concern. Repeat posts and comments do not add people. Likes, votes, unnamed relatives, organizations, and deleted handles are excluded.

Named people in news are counted separately from social accounts. They may overlap. Multiple people can describe the same incident.

Dates based on a discussion rather than a specific comment are labelled. LinkedIn comments without a verifiable calendar date are retained as leads, outside the 24-month counts.

AI suitability is an assessment of the workflow, not a verified root cause, project approval, or promise that AI will solve the concern.
